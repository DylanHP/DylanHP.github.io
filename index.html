About me

Hi, I'm Dylan Zanaglio, an Electronic Engineering student at UNIBS (University of Brescia) with a deep passion for electronics, embedded systems, innovation, and Motorsport.

I am always eager to explore new technologies, electronic design methodologies, and programming techniques. My studies and hands-on experience have allowed me to work on hardware projects, develop embedded firmware, and design circuits.
